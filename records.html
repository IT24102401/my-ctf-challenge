Avalon Historical Society — Field Record#EH-071

Blackstone Hill — Mile 7

Handwritten field record, transcribed from the original survey notebook.

SurveyorElias Vale
Date17 April 1998
MarkerBlackstone Hill, Mile 7

This is the last page recovered from Vale's field notebook. It was found loose, folded inside the cover of the incomplete Eastern Road Survey filing, and was never formally catalogued until this transcription.

"Mile 7 reached at dusk. The old marker still stands, though no one has walked this road in years. The register says the survey ends here. It doesn't. The road does not end here."

Beneath the note, in the same hand, is a short line that appears to be an internal filing code — the kind stamped on archive boxes rather than written by a surveyor in the field. It reads:

NW-7-VALE

No box bearing this code has ever been located in the Society's own storage. Whatever Elias Vale filed under this reference, it left the eastern office with him.

END OF RECORD
