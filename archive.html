Eastern Road Survey

Register of surveying expeditions along the eastern road network, 1994–1999.

The eastern road network was resurveyed annually through the late 1990s following the collapse of the old chancery maps. Four surveyors were assigned to the office over this period. Their filings are listed below.

SurveyorThomas Reed — 1996
SurveyorMarcus Bell — 1997
SurveyorElias Vale — 1998
SurveyorDaniel Cross — 1999

Of these, only the 1998 filing was ever marked incomplete in the office ledger. Its cover sheet reads:

Survey titleEastern Road Survey
SurveyorElias Vale
Date filed17 April 1998
LocationNorthwatch Road
StatusARCHIVED — incomplete

No further pages from this filing survive in the historical society's own holdings. Researchers are directed to the Road & Heritage Register for the corresponding route history.
