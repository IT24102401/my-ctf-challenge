The Forgotten Archivist

A record recovered from the sealed vault beneath the old chancery.

After the king vanished, the royal archivist Elias Vale disappeared as well.

The official records claim that he fled Avalon.

But one surviving note, tucked behind a false shelf panel, suggests otherwise:

“The archivist did not flee east. He followed the road that the king had forbidden.”

Three things were written beneath the note, in a hand different from the first:

NameElias Vale
ReferenceEastern Road Survey
Date17 April 1998

The royal archives themselves contain no further information on the matter. Whatever happened next was never filed — or was filed somewhere it wasn't meant to be found.

Your task: find the place where Elias Vale made his final record.
Everything you need exists somewhere in the public record. Start by searching what you already know.
